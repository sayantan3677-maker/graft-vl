# Hybrid VLM — results and release candidates (2026-11-20)

Architecture (roadmap §3.5): text-only Qwen3 LM inside the Qwen3-VL architecture, with Qwen3-VL's vision encoder, merger and DeepStack.
Training: merger alignment (1,500 steps), then S2 LoRA r32 on the LM (base LM weights frozen). Served natively by vLLM.

## Release candidates

Notes on these numbers:
- Text retention is the change against the original text-only model. It averages MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, IFEval and MATH-500, each in thinking and non-thinking mode, over 2 seeds. Single tasks carry about ±2 points of sampling noise.
- Latency was measured on a shared GPU, so treat it as an upper bound.

## Other checks

**Thinking mode on image QA (40 questions per source):** no gain.
- 4B LoRA 0.5: 55.7 with thinking vs 55.7 without.
- 1.7B: −4.6 to +1.4 points.
- Serve non-thinking by default for image traffic.

**Prompt injection** (120 images with embedded instructions):
- No checkpoint obeyed an injected instruction.
- The injected marker text was echoed in 16% (4B LoRA 0.5), 9% (4B LoRA 1.0) and 34% (1.7B s2000) of answers. It shows up mostly in web-style images.

**vLLM parity (1.7B):**
- Prompt token ids identical in 48/48 cases.
- Agreement with an FP32 reference is the same as HF-BF16's (97.4% vs 97.1% top-1).
- FP8 gives only +5–10% throughput at this size, so serve in BF16.

## Serving checklist

1. `scripts/export_hybrid.py` now runs `scripts/prepare_hybrid_serving.py` automatically. That step writes:
   - the VL-aware Qwen3 template and the nothink template;
   - the 1.6 MP pixel cap;
   - the base LM's `generation_config`.
2. Serve with `--default-chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking": false}'` for interactive and image traffic.
3. `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0` is required on Blackwell (sm_120).
4. Re-measure latency on a dedicated GPU before setting SLOs.

## Known gaps / next steps

1. **Document and chart reading is the main gap:** DocVQA −28, ChartQA −34 and InfoVQA −35 points vs Qwen3-VL-2B. The 4B LM improves general VQA (MMBench +11, MMMU +5) but not ChartQA. Proposed next steps:
   - S3 SFT that is heavier on text-rich and chart data;
   - unfreeze the top ViT blocks;
   - a higher LoRA rank, or full fine-tuning of the 4B with FP32 master weights.
2. **Answer format:** fix the "Answer: X" habit for multiple-choice questions via S3 data (bare-letter targets plus format-following prompts).
3. **Injection echo:** add refusal-to-quote examples for instruction-like text in images to S3.
4. **Licensing:** FineVision sources are still marked "unverified" in `LICENSES.md`. They need sign-off before a product release.
